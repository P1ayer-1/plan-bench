Add currency conversion with historical exchange rates, in a new module `ledger/fx.py`.

## `RateTable`

- `RateTable()` creates an empty table.
- `add(on: date, base: str, quote: str, rate)`: from date `on` onward, 1 unit of `base` = `rate` units of
  `quote`. `rate` may be a `str`, `int` or `Decimal`; store it as an exact `Decimal` (never go through
  `float`). A later `add` for the same pair and an earlier-or-equal date takes effect from its own date.
  Adding the same pair and date twice replaces the earlier rate.
- `rate(base, quote, on: date) -> Decimal`: the exchange rate in effect on date `on`:
  1. If `base == quote`, it's `Decimal(1)`.
  2. Otherwise use the most recent `base→quote` rate dated on or before `on`.
  3. If there's none, use `1 / r` where r is the most recent `quote→base` rate dated on or before `on`.
  4. If there's neither, convert through USD: `rate(base, "USD", on) * rate("USD", quote, on)`, each leg
     looked up with rules 1–3 (no further chaining).
  5. If nothing works, raise `KeyError`.

  Rates are exact `Decimal`s. Never round a rate, or a product of rates, inside `rate()`; do the arithmetic
  with at least 28 significant digits.

## Conversion

- `convert(money: Money, to: str, table: RateTable, on: date) -> Money`: multiply at full precision, then
  round **once** to the cent (half-up, as `Money` already does).
- `Ledger.balance_in(currency: str, table: RateTable, on: date | None = None) -> Money`: the ledger's total
  in `currency`. Convert each transaction at the rate on its own date, or on `on` for every transaction if
  `on` is given. Add up the **unrounded** converted amounts and round once at the end (do not round each
  transaction separately). An empty ledger gives `Money.zero(currency)`. Any missing rate raises `KeyError`.
